bump: minor

The progression-suggestion cards on the Log tab's current routine now have a
working "Apply to note" action. Applying inserts the suggested target into the
current routine and saves it, then shows a clear result for every attempt:
confirmation when the target was added, or an explanation that nothing was
added when the suggestion no longer applies, a save is still in progress, or
the save failed. Applying never dismisses or mutes the suggestion, and a
no-op or failed attempt leaves the note text unchanged.
