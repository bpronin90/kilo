Recovery now quietly fixes a few kinds of leftover inconsistency on your device, such as weeks still active under a deleted block, two blocks active at once, or the same workout note linked twice, without deleting any history. Anything it cannot fix safely, such as a week whose block is missing, stays exactly as it is: Recovery keeps showing the rest of your data with a notice and Retry recovery (which re-checks but may not change the result), only Recovery changes that involve the unresolved data are paused, and the rest of the app keeps working.
