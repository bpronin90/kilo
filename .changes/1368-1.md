Recovery now repairs conflicting saved data on its own, such as a week left behind by a block that was deleted partway or records that arrived from another device out of order. Your notes, baselines, and Recovery history are kept, and a week whose block hasn't downloaded yet is set aside safely until it does. If something still can't be read, Recovery shows that it is unconfirmed and Retry recovery now always leads to a way forward, never a dead end.
