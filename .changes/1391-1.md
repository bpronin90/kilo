Exercise Progress no longer gets stuck on `First session` after a Recovery block or deload. A Track tap is counted against your whole notebook, and Home and Analytics now map that starting point onto the sessions they actually count, so every session logged after you tapped Track is used. When every tracked exercise is still on its first tracked session, Home and Analytics now say how many exercises are establishing a baseline instead of showing 0 · 0 · 0.
