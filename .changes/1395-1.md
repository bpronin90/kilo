Applying a Normalize exercise names reply now carries each renamed exercise's Track setting to its normalized name. A tracked header such as `-DB Bench Press 3x6-8, step up` normalized to `-DB Bench Press 3x6-8` keeps the exercise tracked, and its tracked span still starts where you turned Track on: sessions from before that point stay out of the trend after the merge. If another header or routine still uses the old name, that one stays tracked too. The renamed text and the Track change are saved together; if saving is interrupted, Kilo finishes it the next time it starts or syncs, and a save that fails leaves both unchanged. Exercises already left untracked by an earlier normalization are not repaired automatically.
