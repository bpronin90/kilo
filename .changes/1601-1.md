Weight has a new layout: a compact weigh-in row with a Today date button and an Add note field, a Latest panel with your newest weigh-in, its change and your 7- and 30-day averages (with a link to the full trend in Analytics), a Goal panel with a progress bar when a start weight is saved, and a History table with a date filter.
