bump: minor

Log now supports advancing and manually completing a recovery block: complete a week before adding the next one, attach a new or existing note as each sequential week, unlink the latest week, complete the whole block, and browse completed blocks in a collapsible history. Completing a block with its open current week, and deleting a linked recovery-week note, are now crash-safe: each records its intent before writing, verifies the result against stored data, and resumes automatically after an interrupted attempt, an app restart, or a sync. If a change cannot be finished yet it is shown as pending with a Retry recovery action instead of being reported as done, and a pending change never leaves a recovery week pointing at a deleted note.
