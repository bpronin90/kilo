bump: minor

Added a per-block "Include recovery notes in normal analytics" switch to the active
and completed recovery blocks in the Log tab. It is off by default, so a block's
linked recovery weeks stay out of exercise classifications, progressive-overload
signals, Kilo Max, non-weighted tracked metrics, 1K history, and Home summaries
while remaining fully visible, editable, and present in Recovery Analytics.
Turning it on feeds those notes back through the ordinary aggregations, and each
block honors its own setting independently. Deload exclusions are unchanged.
Restoring a backup now refreshes recovery data on the open screens, and if the
recovery records cannot be read, Home and the affected Analytics sections keep
loading instead of showing totals that include work you chose to exclude.
