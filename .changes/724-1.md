bump: minor

Contained routine and recovery management on the Log tab so the active routine
stays the focus. More Routines is now collapsed by default, showing a routine
count and the latest routine with an expand chevron; its routine cards, "+ New
routine", and "Start recovery block" appear when you expand it. Starting a
recovery block now lives inside expanded routine management instead of the
Recovery section, and is offered only when one can actually be started. The
Recovery section no longer appears at all unless you have an active or recent
recovery block, and a first load no longer briefly flashes a Recovery card. The
"Double-tap to edit" hint text was removed from saved routines while double-tap
editing still works.
