issue: 751
bump: patch

The fatigue check-in now only asks about sessions there is real evidence for,
and only when you finish one. Pressing Skip week, or logging a whole day as
skipped, no longer produces a "you okay?" prompt — a skip is taken as your
statement that nothing happened. Leaving the Log tab mid-edit no longer prompts
either, so the check-in can never ask about work that was not saved. Straight
sets that lose a rep or two near failure, back-off sets below the day's top
weight, and a second-ever session at a new weight are all treated as ordinary
training instead of a decline, and "more skipped than usual" is now measured
against your own usual rate rather than your best-ever session. After a
check-in, the next two sessions are left alone. The prompt no longer appears
over a failed or still-loading read, while another dialog is open, or when
fatigue tracking is off, and it closes without saving anything if any of those
change while it is open. Its wording now describes the session — "Lighter than
usual", "Normal for me", "It was a rough one" — instead of asking how you are.
