bump: patch

A routine's date in `More Routines` is now the day you created it, and stays put when you edit the routine, read a week, sync, or restore a backup; the list and its `Latest:` summary are ordered by that same date. Tapping a Recovery week now opens that week's note right there in the Recovery card — including when the week is your current routine — instead of sending you to `More Routines`, and a week whose note is missing says `Note unavailable` while keeping its `Unlink`. `More Routines` and the `Deload Week` card now stay open or closed as you left them when you switch between Routine and Deload.
