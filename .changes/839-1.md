Recovery: you can now reopen your most recently completed recovery block
(when no other block is active) without losing any week's completion state.
The Log tab shows a "Reopen recovery block" row alongside "Start recovery
block" whenever eligible, and the Analytics evidence card offers the same
action on the newest completed block. The "Complete recovery block"
confirmation no longer falsely claims completion cannot be undone.
