issue: 872
bump: minor

You can now record why a Recovery block started — an optional line of your own
("torn hamstring", "8 weeks off") entered when you start the block and editable
afterwards from Manage block. It appears beside the block on Log and on the
Recovery evidence and completed-block history in Analytics. It is optional
everywhere, changes nothing about how recovery weeks are compared or counted,
and blocks started before this update simply have none.
