issue: 872
bump: minor

You can now record why a Recovery block started — an optional line of your own
("torn hamstring", "8 weeks off") entered when you start the block and editable
afterwards, either from Manage block on Log or straight from the Recovery
evidence card in Analytics, including on a block you have already finished. It
appears beside the block on Log and on the Recovery evidence and completed-block
history in Analytics. It is optional
everywhere, changes nothing about how recovery weeks are compared or counted,
and blocks started before this update simply have none.
