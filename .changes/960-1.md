bump: minor

Progression suggestions can now be turned on from Settings (default off) and,
once on, surface as explainable cards on the Log current-routine view and the
Analytics strength section. Each card shows the observed evidence, the
heuristic recommendation, and the deterministic explanation from the existing
rule engine, and is clearly marked as a conditional prompt rather than a
guaranteed prescription. A card can be dismissed for the current view or an
exercise can be muted so its suggestions stay hidden in both places until it is
unmuted. Nothing here edits, prefills, or rewrites workout-note text — applying
a suggestion is still manual.
