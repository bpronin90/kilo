## Brand & Style

The design system embodies athletic utility, architectural precision, and high-performance discipline. It rejects decorative gym clichés—there are no distressed textures, faux-leather surfaces, or hyper-aggressive neon glows, nor is it a sterile dark-mode terminal IDE or artisanal warm-oatmeal notebook. Instead, it operates with the rigor of professional sports timing equipment and technical field manuals: crisp, objective, deliberate, and exceptionally clean.

The emotional signature is focused confidence, momentum, and operational clarity. Users interact with a tactilely grounded interface where every set, rep, kilo, and split is recorded with absolute zero friction. The design movement merges Modern Utilitarianism with Crisp Swiss Precision: pure flat surfaces, sharp boundaries, high legibility under active physical strain, and resolute structural alignment.

## Colors

The foundation is built on an architectural cool mist slate canvas (`#eaedf0`), delivering depth without murkiness or warm sepia undertones. Elevated surfaces and primary data cards rely on pure crisp white (`#ffffff`) to create immediate figure-ground contrast. Structural separations are maintained using razor-sharp steel dividers (`#cbd5e1` and `#e2e8f0`).

Primary ink is obsidian navy-charcoal (`#0f172a`), providing optimal daylight readability and extreme high contrast without the harsh optical vibration of pure `#000000`. 

- **Primary Accent (`#1d4ed8` / `#2563eb`):** Electric Lapis / Cobalt. Used exclusively for focused active sets, running stopwatches, interactive controls, and immediate focal operations.
- **Secondary Accent (`#059669`):** Verified Athletic Emerald. Dedicated to completed sets, target achievements, personal records, and confirmed checkmarks.
- **Tertiary Tone (`#475569`):** Structural Slate. Used for secondary annotations, metric units (kg, reps), column headers, and structural grid rules.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base App Canvas: `#eaedf0`
  - Elevated Tile / Card Surface: `#ffffff`
  - Subtle Inset / Metric Track: `#f1f5f9`
  - Muted Inactive Borders: `#e2e8f0`
  - Focused / Active Borders: `#0f172a`

## Typography

Typography establishes an unwavering division of labor:
1. **Space Grotesk** governs all linguistic structure: titles, exercise taxonomy, movement instructions, and UI commands. Its technical geometric construction provides an athletic and architectural posture without feeling decorative.
2. **JetBrains Mono** governs all quantitative and temporal values: kilos, plate breakdowns, target repetitions, RPE ratings, and chronometer digits. Tabular figures prevent layout jumping during high-speed logging and active stopwatch ticking.

All numeric metric indicators (`label-sm`, `label-md`) default to uppercase tracking for rapid scan-readability during elevated heart-rate sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout is structured around an ultra-compact, high-density rhythm designed for handheld operation and table-mounted glanceability:
- **Mobile (Phone):** Single-column stack spanning 100% width with `1rem` outer screen safety margins. Rep and set matrices adapt as tabular data sheets with `0.5rem` row gaps and `0.75rem` column gutters.
- **Tablet / Desktop:** Max container constraints at `1024px` centered on canvas, transitioning into a multi-pane split layout: Left pane (40%) anchors routine telemetry and resting timers; Right pane (60%) anchors the workout logbook and weight calculation sheets.

Component internal padding favors compact vertical breathing space (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) paired with generous horizontal hit regions (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to facilitate rapid, unencumbered thumb input.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely discards fuzzy, ambient drop shadows and skeuomorphic gradients in favor of crisp architectural layering and structural line work:
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat cool mist slate (`#eaedf0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Solid crisp white (`#ffffff`) bounded by a hairline structural rule (`1px solid #cbd5e1`).
- **Level 2 (Active Focus / Selected Item):** Raised white with an intense 1.5px steel border (`#0f172a`), creating immediate mechanical presence without spatial distortion.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Overlays & Modals):** Pure white container flanked by an offset, high-definition structural keyline: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #cbd5e1` coupled with a solid `1px solid #0f172a` boundary.
- **Depressed / Inactive Track:** Inset containers (e.g., historical logs, rest period bars) drop into a muted fill (`#f1f5f9`) bordered by `#e2e8f0`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is strictly disciplined, using an architectural curvature restricted to 4px (`roundedness: 1`), scaling to a maximum of 6px (`rounded-lg`) for full-width cards. 

Pill shapes, bubble corners, and arbitrary circular surfaces are prohibited—buttons, inputs, and badges maintain exact, chiseled corners. This restraint guarantees that the visual vocabulary feels like professional instrumentation, maximizing usable internal canvas area for dense workout data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0f172a`, text `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#0f172a`, radius 4px. Active state transitions to `#1d4ed8`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, radius 4px. Hover/Press shifts border to `#0f172a`.
- **Completion (Mark Set Done):** Solid `#059669` fill, white tabular monospaced text, instantaneous tactile snap on tap.

### Exercise Cards & Tables
- Encased in white `#ffffff`, 1px solid `#cbd5e1` outline, 6px radius.
- Table headers utilize `label-sm` in `#475569` with bottom divider `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- Rows alternate no zebra stripes; focus state outlines the row with a 1.5px solid `#1d4ed8` perimeter.

### Chips & Badges
- **PR / Record:** Mint background (`#ecfdf5`), border 1px solid `#059669`, text `#065f46`, font `label-sm`.
- **Set Tag (Warmup, Drop, Failure):** Cool slate background (`#f1f5f9`), border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, text `#334155`.

### Input Fields (Kilos & Reps)
- JetBrains Mono numeric inputs with centered alignment.
- Height: 42px. Background: `#ffffff`. Border: 1px solid `#cbd5e1`. Radius: 4px.
- Focus: 2px solid `#1d4ed8` with no peripheral glow or ring spread.

### Stopwatch & Interval Timer
- Full-width modular HUD ribbon. Background `#0f172a`, text `#ffffff`.
- Dynamic countdown highlighted via an edge-to-edge solid `#1d4ed8` progress hairline (2px height).
